☕ The Latte Paws Story

Latte Paws was born from a simple belief: our dogs deserve to be celebrated every single day.

Based in Hong Kong, we create beautiful, whimsical pet printables that help dog parents capture special moments — from spooky Halloween photos to cozy Christmas mornings.

Every design is crafted with love, tested on our own four-legged taste testers, and delivered instantly to your inbox. Zero inventory, zero waste, 100% tail wags. 🐕

❓ Questions?

We've got answers.

How do I get my printables? ▼
After purchase, you'll receive an instant download link via email. Print at home or at your local print shop!
What format are the files? ▼
All printables come in high-resolution PDF format (8.5x11 inches / A4 compatible). Ready to print!
Can I use these for commercial purposes? ▼
Our printables are for personal use only. Please contact us for commercial licensing inquiries.
Do you ship physical products? ▼
Currently we offer digital printables only. We're exploring print-on-demand products (mugs, tees, hoodies) — stay tuned!
What's your return policy? ▼
Due to the digital nature of our products, all sales are final. But if you have any issues, email us and we'll make it right!
